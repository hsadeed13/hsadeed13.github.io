01What it does

Two sides of one application. Visitors browse reviews and click through to Amazon; the owner runs everything from a password-protected admin area with no code changes.

For visitors

  • Instant search with photo suggestions while typing, and a full-screen search on phones.
  • Category pages with subcategory tabs, plus rating, brand and sort filters that update without a page reload.
  • Review pages with a swipeable photo gallery, pros and cons, the full article and any number of YouTube videos.
  • Landing pages for a single product or a ranked "Best of" guide for a category.
  • Recently viewed products, a share button and a sticky "Check Price" bar on phones.
  • Amazon policy compliant: disclosure on every page, no prices, ratings shown as "Our score".

For the owner

  • Dashboard with a 7-day chart of clicks to Amazon and the most-clicked products.
  • Categories with one level of subcategories: add, edit, delete, reorder, show or hide.
  • Products with multiple photos, a rich-text review, pros and cons, badges, SEO fields and YouTube videos.
  • Quick rating edits straight from the product list, saved without leaving the page.
  • One-click landing pages from any product or category, with hand-picked and ranked products.
  • Settings for the site name, home page text, Amazon tracking ID and admin login.

05How it's built

A standard Laravel structure (models, controllers, Blade views, migrations) with a few problems worth solving properly.

Click tracking

Every "Check Price" button goes through /go/{product}, which logs the click and its source, then adds the Amazon tracking ID if the link lacks one.

One landing page model

A polymorphic landable relation lets one table serve both product and category pages, with a pivot table for hand-picked, ranked products.

Safe rich text

Review articles are sanitised on the server with an allowlist of tags and attributes before they are stored, so pasted HTML can't inject scripts.

Filters without reloads

Filter links are ordinary URLs. JavaScript fetches the next page, swaps only the product list and updates the address bar, and the page still works without JavaScript.

YouTube links in any form

Watch, share, Shorts, embed and live links are parsed to a video ID. Videos load only when clicked, from the privacy-enhanced player.

Data integrity

Foreign keys stop a category being deleted while it still has products or subcategories, and removing a photo also deletes its file from disk.
